Academic Readmission & Deferment Guides

Published: September 28, 2026

If you are returning from an official deferment period, you must submit your signed approval letters to the Academic Registry desk. New applicants seeking to defer must initiate applications at the Admissions desk before the registration freeze window closes.

Finance & Fees Clearance Timelines

Published: September 28, 2026

Ensure your bank slips are tracked directly in the university ERP database. Any financial holds on your portal must be addressed at the Finance Block accounts window before exam cards can be printed.

Emergency Alerts

Security: Lost or stolen items should be reported to the Security Command desk within 24 hours.

Health: The University Health Clinic is open 24/7 for acute medical concerns or physical injuries.
