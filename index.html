StudentSupport

StudentSupport is a system that helps university students identify their problems and find the right university support service.

Core Support Directory Features

Select your challenge category to discover where you need to go on campus:

  • Finance & Fees Clearance Desk: Routes students dealing with fee statements, bank slips, and financial holds to the Finance Block.
  • Academic Affairs Registry: Guides students facing issues with missing marks, course registration, timetable clashes, or returning students processing their readmission after a deferment break to the Registrar's Office.
  • Guidance & Counselling Unit: Helps students looking for personal mentorship, stress management, or wellness support find the Counselling Centre.
  • Admissions & Student Affairs: Directs newly arriving students, as well as admitted applicants who initially failed to join due to unavoidable factors and need to process their formal deferment applications to the right desk for admission files, hostel bookings, and orientation materials.
  • ICT Helpdesk Support: Points students struggling with portal logins, university Wi-Fi, or corporate email activation to the ICT Center.
  • Campus Security & Law Enforcement: Directs students dealing with lost belongings, physical harassment, trespassing, or disciplinary offenses directly to the Campus Security Command Center.
  • University Health Services & Clinic: Provides immediate directional routing for students experiencing sudden illnesses, physical injuries, or medical emergencies needing urgent clinical care.

StudentSupport Technical Architecture

The structural layout of our application deployment framework:

  • Front-end (User Interface)
    • HTML5 (Page Structure & Navigation Directories)
    • CSS3 (Responsive Layout Styling & UI Design)
    • JavaScript (Interactive Routing & Forms Processing)
  • Back-end (Server & Database Management)
    • PHP (Dynamic Routing and Form Action Scripts)
    • MySQL (Campus Records & Support Contacts Database)
